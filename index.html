Your Airthings air quality,
on your Home Screen.

LuftDings puts your Airthings sensor readings into iOS widgets — so you can see your air at a glance without opening an app.

Every reading

Radon, CO₂, VOC, temperature, humidity, pressure — and PM2.5 on supported devices like the View Plus.

Widgets, finally

Small, medium, and large Home Screen widgets showing the sensors that matter to you, with a clear “last updated” time.

Works with WiFi devices

Reads your data through the Airthings Cloud API, so it works across all device types — including the WiFi View series.

Private by design

No account with us, no backend, no tracking. Your credentials stay in the iOS Keychain on your device.

How it connects

LuftDings reads your sensor data using your own Airthings API key (Bring Your Own API Key). Every Airthings account includes a free API key — you create it once in your Airthings dashboard and paste it into the app. The app then talks directly to the Airthings Cloud API from your device.

An Airthings account and API key are required to show live data. Without them, you can still explore the whole app in demo mode with realistic sample data. LuftDings is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Airthings.
