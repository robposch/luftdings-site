Privacy Policy

Short version: LuftDings does not collect your data. There is no LuftDings account and no LuftDings server. Everything happens on your device.

Last updated: 21 June 2026

Who we are

LuftDings (“the app”, “we”) is an independent, unofficial iOS app that displays air-quality readings from your Airthings sensors. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Airthings AS.

What we collect

Nothing. LuftDings has no backend server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not create an account for you, and we never receive, see, or store any of your information.

Your Airthings credentials

To show live data, you provide your own Airthings API credentials (a client ID and secret you generate in your Airthings account — “Bring Your Own API Key”). These credentials:

  • are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device;
  • are shared with the LuftDings widget through a private, on-device App Group container so the widget can refresh;
  • are never transmitted to us or to any server we control — we operate no servers.

Your air-quality data

When you use the app with live data, LuftDings sends your Airthings credentials directly from your device to the Airthings Cloud API and receives your latest sensor readings in return. Those readings are cached locally on your device (in the App Group container) so the widgets can display them and show a “last updated” time. This data is not sent anywhere else.

Your use of the Airthings Cloud API is governed by Airthings’ own terms and privacy policy. We have no control over and take no responsibility for Airthings’ handling of your data.

Demo mode

If you have not entered credentials, the app can run in a demo mode using built-in, fictional sample data. No network requests are made and no data leaves your device in demo mode.

Apple App Store “data not collected”

Consistent with the above, LuftDings declares “Data Not Collected” on its App Store privacy label.

Children

LuftDings is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.
